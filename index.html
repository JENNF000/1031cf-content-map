              Comments

                Statuses & labels

                Both are yours to shape. Rename anything, change a colour, add your own, or remove what you don't use — removing takes it off every page.

                Statuses

                One per page, and the order here is the order they appear on a page. These are the steps of your workflow, so arrange them however you actually work.

                Labels

                Any number per page. The ones marked auto are applied by the build — Review and Remove came from your workbook, the rest are computed from the SEMrush and crawl data. They're editable and removable like all the others.

                Update redirect status

                For when you've just shipped redirects and want the map to reflect it now. Costs no SEMrush units and waits for no rebuild.

                Paste the URLs

                Full URLs or paths, one per line or comma separated, trailing slash optional — whatever your redirect plugin exports. Anything not in the inventory is listed back to you rather than silently dropped.

                Or check the whole site

                The app is being served from the site's own origin, so it can test every URL itself.

                Why isn't there a button to scan the site from here? Because the browser won't allow it: the app is on github.io and your site is on 1031crowdfunding.com, and cross-origin requests can't reveal whether a URL redirects. Ask me and I'll run a verified sweep in a tab on your own site — that's how the current redirect map was built. Serving this app from your own domain would make the scan work natively.

                Sync settings

                Your labels, comments and moves are always saved on this device first. Connecting a GitHub repo also pushes them to annotations.json, so your phone and laptop stay in step — and so the weekly rebuild can read what you've marked.

                Use a fine-grained personal access token scoped to this one repository, with Contents: Read and write and nothing else. It's stored in this browser only — never in the repo, never sent anywhere but github.com. Revoke it any time from your GitHub settings.
